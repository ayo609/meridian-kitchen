1. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account detailsFull name, email, phone number, password

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process orders and reservations
  • To send order confirmations and reservation updates
  • To send promotional offers, only if you've opted in
  • To improve our menu, service, and website experience

3. How We Store Your Information

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (a Google Cloud platform), including Firebase Authentication for login credentials and Firestore for your profile and order data. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Limited data may be shared with trusted service providers strictly for the purpose of fulfilling orders (e.g. payment processors, delivery partners), and only to the extent necessary.

5. Your Choices

  • You can update or correct your account details at any time.
  • You can opt out of marketing messages at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service and comply with legal obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, please reach out via the contact details listed on our homepage.
